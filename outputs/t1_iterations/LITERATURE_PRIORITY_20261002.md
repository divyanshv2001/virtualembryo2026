## Decision and prospective validation

First implement the already declared actual-submission coverage audit. It must determine how much of the official 32,285-gene anchor the current model can update, and verify actual one-day timing/provenance. The literature does not establish the cause of 52.16.

Then, if coverage supports a dynamics test, predeclare a bounded soft-OT flow-matching adaptation: reuse the same past-fitted representation and full-panel decoder as the incumbent, fit minibatch soft transition targets between eligible earlier stages, normalize velocities by actual elapsed days, and add a distribution loss only at observed training stages. A matched incumbent-objective control is essential to isolate the new training objective. Bidirectional consistency is a later isolated ablation, not an excuse to use hidden future cells.

Use a rolling one-day forecast wherever eligible observations allow it, fixed seeds/splits, copy and incumbent controls, unchanged scorer/calibration, all four raw metrics and skills, and actual gene-space predictions. All preprocessing/pairing/regularization inputs must be at or before the fold cutoff. Reject a failed full-panel forecast even if latent distances improve. Preserve DE sign/magnitude, MMD and variogram regressions; do not optimize a selected-gene proxy and call it a benchmark gain.
