# Cross-examination protocol

Actual initial reviews and proposals are visible before peer responses. Pairs: devbio↔neuralode, scgenomics↔spatialtx, grn↔causal, sysbio↔sciml, genmodel↔diffusion, gnn↔spatial3d, replearn↔biovalid, stats↔benchdesign. Each expert records the actual peer claim, challenges it with evidence, identifies missing measurements and a discriminating experiment, and gives a provisional disposition. Each saves its own debate artifact and updates its own proposal peer_responses.

The four-thread concurrency limit sometimes rejects sending to an idle peer, because retaining/reactivating the peer would consume a slot. Those failures are real and are not relabeled as delivery. The orchestrator routes the attributed, written challenge to the counterpart. Shared artifacts are the substantive dialogue record. Where direct messages succeed, the recipient consumes them on a subsequent turn. No external messaging tools are involved.

Debate resolves logical scope or narrows methodological claims; it cannot resolve absent biological measurements through agreement. Findings requiring experiment remain open. Final disagreement_registry.json records both positions and the required evidence, including disagreements narrowed rather than settled.
