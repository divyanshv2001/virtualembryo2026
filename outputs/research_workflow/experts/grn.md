# Gene Regulatory Networks — initial independent review

My worldview is that a useful perturbation predictor need not recover a causal regulatory network. A graph is first an inductive bias; calling an edge regulatory requires evidence separating direct regulation from common developmental state, cell composition, indirect signaling and technical effects. My preferred evidence is an independently replicated intervention, early response measurements, cell-type-specific chromatin occupancy and rescue. Edge stability or coexpression alone is insufficient. This follows the local mandate to challenge regulatory causality in `Neurl IPS 2026/agenticprompt:155–167` and the explicit warning in `Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:113`.

The audited state and inventory contain no biological data, fitted network, perturbation output or experiment. The persona's mandate at `Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:182–190` refers to absent model/audit/literature files. Consequently, the Mab21l2 ranking assertion and historical graph-concordance test are UNVERIFIED, not closed findings. The supplied prompts propose network experiments (`Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:361–385`); they do not implement them. A strength is that the prompts already request no-prior versus known-prior comparisons and randomized controls (`Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:2040–2054`). The weakness is that such comparisons have not been run.

The [official task definition](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/tasks), inspected 2026-09-27, establishes a 500-gene MERFISH panel and one released knockout, Mab21l2 at E9.5, with Gata4 and β-catenin targets at E8.75. This supports a prediction problem, not identification of all regulatory edges. Thousands of cells from one intervention do not create thousands of independent intervention identities. A knockout response can include developmental delay, missing cell types, survival effects and indirect transcriptional change. Inferring a distinct Gata4 or β-catenin effect from Mab21l2 therefore requires an untested transfer assumption. A later-stage intervention is additionally confounded with developmental context when transferred to an earlier stage.

The panel is an observation operator, not a complete biological state. Its actual gene list is absent locally. I cannot confirm which transcription factors, cofactors or pathway readouts are measured; in particular the persona's Axin2 absence assertion is not independent verification. Dropping unmeasured regulators can make association edges represent latent common causes or indirect paths. A graph restricted to the panel may also disconnect a held-out perturbation from relevant targets. Audit gene identifiers, panel inclusion and prior coverage before selecting a network architecture. Do not equate β-catenin transcript abundance with the full activity of the corresponding signaling system.

Two primary references constrain the interpretation. [Pratapa et al., BEELINE, Nature Methods 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-019-0690-6), peer reviewed, was verified at abstract level; the publisher full-text open failed. Its benchmark reports moderate precision and favors methods that do not require pseudotime in its evaluated settings. It motivates skeptical edge evaluation, not a universal conclusion that every network method fails. [Roohani, Huang and Leskovec, GEARS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-023-01905-6), peer reviewed, published online 2023 and in Nature Biotechnology volume 42 in 2024, was inspected in full text. It uses coexpression and Gene Ontology graphs, with many perturbation identities in cell-line screens. Its discussion requires matching cell type or condition for reliable prediction. It supplies a candidate prior-based method, not evidence that one embryonic knockout suffices or that its graphs identify direct regulation. These two papers are methodological precedents; no performance transfers to this absent implementation.

The persona's blanket external-prior prohibition is outdated. The [current rules, section 10](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules) permit disclosed external resources but exclude protected stage/genotype measurements and pretraining containing them. Log dataset accession, species, cell type, developmental stage, genotype, allele, perturbation, filtering, model ancestry and licences. Published Gata4 binding information is not automatically eligible target evidence: its derivation must be checked. A generic ontology edge and measured held-out knockout response have different provenance risks.

My first concrete improvement is a matched-capacity experiment: no graph, training-only coexpression, an eligible prior, and repeated degree-preserving randomized priors, with identical tuning budgets and independent-embryo splits. Evaluate perturbation differences using de_score, de_direction, severity_slope and mmd_u. Prespecify a practical margin before evaluating. If the true graph cannot exceed randomized graphs, reject biological edge identity as the explanation for any gain; regularization remains plausible. Even a positive result establishes predictive dependence, not causality.

The discriminating biological experiment is separate from competition prediction: acute, cell-type-restricted perturbation with an early full-transcriptome readout, matched controls, occupancy measurement and rescue, replicated across embryos. Test whether a predicted target changes before composition shifts and whether rescuing the proposed regulator reverses the effect. Keep protected knockout experiments outside eligible competition training. I explicitly reject treating attention weights or graph concordance as causal validation, and reject rescaling Mab21l2 deltas into Gata4/β-catenin responses without an independent test.

Confidence is high in the evidence limitations and moderate in the utility of the proposed controls; confidence in a graph improving prediction is low. Questions for **sysbio**: which response survives cell-composition decomposition? For **causal**: what direct-effect estimand and intervention assumptions are identifiable? For **gnn**: can degree, capacity and smoothing explain your expected graph benefit? These are pending questions, not simulated peer responses.
