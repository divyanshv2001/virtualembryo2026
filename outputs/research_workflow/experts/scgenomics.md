# Single-Cell Genomics Methodologist — initial review

My worldview is that an expression matrix is an observation of a sampled tissue through an assay, not a direct readout of developmental state. I prioritize measurement provenance, independently sampled embryos, cell-type conditional distributions and reproducible preprocessing over latent-space beauty. The persona lens at `Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:84` is useful, but its historical normalization-gap and pseudobulk-win premises are unverified: their cited source files are absent. I read `outputs/research_workflow/PROJECT_STATE.md` and `inventory.json`; neither records biological data or executed biological experiments. Consequently every proposed improvement below remains a hypothesis.

The strongest existing safeguard is the prompt's explicit biological-unit requirement (`Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:1037`) and leakage checklist (`:1554`–`:1578`). The submission validator also checks gene order, finite nonnegative expression and coordinates (`Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:128`). These are necessary but insufficient. `_dense` consumes `.X` without establishing its transformation (`:121`); missing `common` and `scoring` imports prevent direct verification of metric preprocessing. `_proxy_score` takes the first 150 cells (`:176`–`:181`), so cell ordering could select an anatomical region or one sample and distort distributional evidence. Hardcoded board numbers at `:188`–`:196` are assertions, not observed scientific results here.

The official [data documentation](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/data), inspected full-text on 2026-09-27, distinguishes whole-transcriptome dissociated RNA from a measured 500-gene MERFISH panel, describes normalized `.X`, local embryo frames, and warns that Task 1 heart-centred dissections vary with developmental stage and annotation vocabulary. These statements make a shared count likelihood unsafe by default. The same page contains an external-data sentence inconsistent with the newer rules summarized in project state; prospective restrictions should follow [official rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules), not that sentence. I infer no hidden target composition from released examples.

Specific failure modes follow from this measurement distinction. Dissociation may alter the recovered mixture and its expression states; MERFISH requires transcript decoding, segmentation and section reconstruction. A targeted panel total is not a whole-transcriptome library size. A score improvement in global pseudobulk can arise solely from shifting cell proportions. Conversely, forcing proportion matching can erase a real developmental transition. A label absent at one stage can reflect annotation naming rather than biological disappearance. Stage, batch and embryo effects become inseparable when each stage supplies only one sample; batch correction cannot identify the missing experimental contrast.

Three precise searches were run, with primary-source follow-up. Hafemeister and Satija, *Genome Biology* (2019), [published method](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6927181/), full text verified, model UMI depth using regularized negative-binomial regression and show overfitting risks in unconstrained models. This supports depth diagnostics, not automatic application of sctransform to normalized challenge inputs. Zhang et al., *Nature* (2021), [published MERFISH atlas](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03705-x), full methods verified, assign decoded molecules to segmented cells and explicitly address section volume and experimental normalization. That illustrates assay dependence, not this embryo dataset's exact preprocessing. Squair et al., *Nature Communications* (2021), [published study, full PDF verified](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25960-2.pdf), show that preserving biological replicates matters for differential-expression inference. It does not establish that one particular pseudobulk test is universally best.

I propose a prospective measurement contract before fitting: record assay, tissue coverage, panel, normalization recipe, available raw layers, section/sample/embryo keys, provenance of labels and embeddings, and exact scorer representation. Use composite sample-plus-cell keys internally; neither row names nor cross-stage labels identify lineages. Preserve the supplied expression representation for artifact writing. If raw counts are absent, do not invent them by exponentiating an incompletely specified normalization.

Next, evaluate composition and within-type state separately. On released data only, compare persistence, proportion-only and within-type-expression-only baselines, plus their combination. Report pooled and cell-type conditional discrepancies, pseudobulk direction, and sample-balanced uncertainty. Harmonize labels through an explicit auditable mapping with unknown categories. Use embryo-level holdouts and bootstrap where sample IDs exist; otherwise report descriptive sensitivity and the identifiability limit. Replace first-row proxies with seeded, sample-balanced repeated sampling. Train normalization, feature selection and classifier fitting within each training fold. Reserve a stage holdout to test temporal generalization; do not confuse it with independent-embryo replication.

I reject a tempting global pseudobulk calibration shift learned across MERFISH and dissociated RNA: it can absorb tissue coverage, assay and mixture differences while appearing to improve an aggregate metric. Confidence is high that the contracts and sampling checks are necessary, moderate that mixture decomposition will diagnose useful errors, and low that any model will improve hidden-board performance because no data or implemented model exists. For stats: how should uncertainty be reported if embryo identifiers are absent? For benchdesign: what split simultaneously tests stage generalization and prevents sample leakage? For replearn: how will representation alignment preserve stage-specific populations while separating platform effects?
