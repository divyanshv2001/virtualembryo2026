# Statistics initial review

My worldview is that uncertainty must follow the sampling and selection design. My preferred evidence is a frozen analysis evaluated on independent embryos with a traceable stage, litter, batch and genotype manifest. Cells, random seeds, and sixteen reviewers sharing one briefing are not sixteen independent biological experiments. The persona lens at `Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:398–415` is useful, but its asserted historical bootstrap failure has no supporting experiment here; I do not adopt the fictional coauthor identity or historical score as evidence.

The audit finds no biological model, released measurements, or predictive results. Actual evaluator code caps the diagnostic at 150 cells and slices the first rows (`Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:175–185`). Ordered tissue acquisition, cell-type sorting, or file concatenation could therefore change the apparent model comparison. This is a concrete implementation weakness; claiming its numerical impact would require data. The proxy specifications at lines 150–159 name endpoint comparisons rather than establishing the training exclusions or interpolation brackets. A filename called validation does not prove a valid forecast.

The strongest feature of `outputs/research_workflow/EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md:25–35` is its honest feasibility analysis. T1's two stages cannot supply both two-point trend fitting and an independent temporal test. T2's three stages permit one outer proxy, but generally cannot support nested tuning of the same two-stage temporal model. T3's single released perturbation identity does not validate unseen-gene transfer. Freeze tuning from training evidence or a separately eligible resource, and log every candidate considered. Residual-guided architecture selection using the outer target would turn that target into development data, even with equal tuning budgets; a fresh eligible evaluation would then be needed.

The unit of analysis for predictive comparisons is the target embryo, with both models evaluated against that same embryo. This is paired model evaluation, not longitudinal pairing of embryos destroyed at successive stages. Shared litters or jointly processed batches may require higher-level clustering; equal embryo weights alone do not establish independence. With one embryo per stage, embryo identity and stage are confounded. Neither random cell splits nor repeated sampling seeds identify between-embryo variability. Separate sampling Monte Carlo variability, optimizer/refit variability, and biological variation; report the first two conditionally when the third is unavailable. Bootstrap intervals from cells may describe a declared conditional measurement procedure, but cannot become population confidence intervals.

The primary combined-versus-composition-only raw `mmd_u` contrast at design line 33 is sensible for isolating added within-state benefit, provided task, horizon, scorer version, cell count and sign convention are frozen. Define improvement as MMD(composition) minus MMD(combined). MMD can improve while directional, co-expression, geometry or neighborhood behavior deteriorates. The design's phrase “without degrading” at line 7 requires explicit guardrail margins and a decision rule; nonsignificant secondary differences do not demonstrate noninferiority. Report all official raw components and the correctly weighted calibrated composite, while maintaining a separate scientific primary estimand. The [official T2 scoring page](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/evaluation?section=scoring&task=2) specifies expression change, cell-state distribution, shape/scale and local organization groups; [T3 scoring](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/evaluation?section=scoring&task=3) emphasizes recovery, direction, severity and distribution. T1's scoring URL failed twice in this review, so I do not claim independent verification of its complete weights.

For replicated planning, estimate the across-target-embryo variance of the paired model difference under the intended acquisition design. Design line 35's normal approximation is only a planning aid: if the success threshold is a positive practical margin, the denominator must reflect the anticipated effect above that threshold, rather than silently treating the threshold as the anticipated alternative against zero. Small pilots warrant sensitivity ranges, cluster-aware simulation and explicit allocation across stages; no numerical power claim is available now.

Relevant primary literature is [Zimmerman, Espeland and Langefeld, Nature Communications, 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21038-1), published, full-text inspected: it documents dependence among cells and motivates accounting for biological individuals. It does not certify a particular mixed model for this missing dataset. [Gretton et al., JMLR, 2012](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume13/gretton12a/gretton12a.pdf), published, full-text inspected, develops kernel two-sample tests; its ordinary sampling assumptions do not erase embryo clustering. [Cawley and Talbot, JMLR, 2010](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume11/cawley10a/cawley10a.pdf), published, full-text inspected, demonstrates selection-induced evaluation optimism. These establish methodological cautions, not project performance.

I explicitly reject “more seeds until five successes” as evidence of biological generalization. I also reject treating critic acceptance as empirical calibration: `Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/memory/update.py:86–122` maps verdicts to supported/falsified and binary sustained counts without experimental outcomes. Preserve separate deliberation and experiment statuses.

Validation requirements are a sample manifest, frozen split/tuning ledger, whole-cloud paired sampling, versioned complete scoring, and either independent embryos or a clearly descriptive report. Confidence is high in these design critiques because they follow inspected code and sampling logic; confidence in any model's benefit is unassessed. Questions: **benchdesign**, what exact task/horizon and guardrail margins will be frozen? **scgenomics**, can embryo/litter identifiers be recovered without inferring them from expression? **causal**, which genotype estimand distinguishes total developmental delay from maturity-conditioned effects?
