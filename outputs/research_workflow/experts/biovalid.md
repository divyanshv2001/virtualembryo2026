# Biological validation review — biovalid — initial round

My worldview is that every biological claim must name an observation that could contradict it. My preferred evidence is a replicated embryo-level contrast measured by an assay independent of the prediction panel. Expression similarity, lineage ancestry, compartment abundance, anatomical structure and direct regulation require different observations. A plausible developmental narrative does not connect those evidential gaps.

I read PROJECT_STATE.md and inventory.json in outputs/research_workflow before reviewing source. No biological data, models or results exist here. The historical persona in Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:445–466 asserts a local knockout and a retired PRDM1 check, but its mandate paths are absent. I therefore cannot certify either historical assertion. There is no local beta-catenin validation either. Missing measurements mean unknown, not a negative biological result.

The strongest existing design feature is the explicit composition/state factorial in outputs/research_workflow/EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md:11–19. A pooled expression shift can arise when cell proportions change while every cell state remains unchanged. Conversely, matching proportions can conceal erroneous within-compartment states. Require both independently counted compartment abundance and within-compartment expression contrasts. Counts should include total tissue or compartment cellularity where feasible: proportions are constrained to sum to one, so an apparent expansion may represent loss elsewhere. Independent imaging or stereological counts can expose dissociation or capture bias. State annotations must be frozen from eligible training evidence and include uncertain assignments.

The source also contains a sampling hazard. Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:175–185 takes the first at most 150 rows, including spatial rows. Ordered cells can systematically omit compartments and misrepresent expression-position relationships. Its hardcoded scores at :188–196 and candidate-name evidence gate at :226–235 do not provide biological provenance. No local assay can be reconstructed from those numbers. A future diagnostic must preserve expression-coordinate pairing, use sample-balanced whole-cloud sampling and report seed sensitivity; these checks establish diagnostic stability rather than model validity.

EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md:33–35 correctly treats embryos as biological replicates. Add an explicit embryo/litter/batch manifest and an assay allocation plan before obtaining outcomes. Splitting cells from one embryo or repeatedly sampling its rows cannot estimate between-embryo variability. If stage and batch coincide, interpretation remains confounded even with many cells. Independent embryos across litters and processing batches, randomized assay order, blinded annotation and matched chronological-age controls are prerequisites for a generalization claim. A maturity-adjusted analysis addresses a different estimand: delay is part of a genotype's total effect, not an automatic nuisance to remove.

Anatomical fidelity requires registered independent imaging with verified physical axes and units, prespecified domain boundaries and topology or organ relationships. Expression panels may miss architecture. Coordinate-bearing predictions alone do not prove anatomy, and a metric sensitive to point distributions may reward size without repairing tissue organization. Report physical scale separately from registration. The challenge's T3 geometry is unscored; anatomical assays are scientific endpoints, not promised score improvements.

For direct versus indirect knockout effects, late whole-embryo expression is inadequate. In a separate eligible scientific study, distinguish early changes preceding composition loss from downstream consequences using timed, cell-type-restricted perturbations, occupancy/accessibility evidence, and appropriate controls. Binding alone does not establish functional regulation. Rescue should verify allele, dosage, temporal expression, compartment localization and restored protein function, and compare both molecular and anatomical endpoints. A compartment-restricted rescue can test tissue dependence but cannot by itself identify every direct target. Traced ancestry requires heritable lineage labels or validated live tracking; a transcriptional trajectory or transport coupling is not a lineage observation.

Relevant literature: Squair et al., “Confronting false discoveries in single-cell differential expression,” Nature Communications (2021), published article, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25960-2. The indexed publisher excerpts support replicate-aware analysis; direct full-text opening failed, so verification is search-snippet-only. Raj et al., “Simultaneous single-cell profiling of lineages and cell types in the vertebrate brain,” Nature Biotechnology (2018), published article, https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt.4103, abstract-only verification, demonstrates paired lineage recording and transcriptomics in zebrafish. This is methodological precedent, not validation of the absent project or a ready mouse protocol.

I reject literature-targeted expression edits for protected knockout predictions: they could insert hidden response measurements and cannot establish causal transfer. Official rules section 10, read directly at https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules, permit disclosed external resources but exclude protected windows/genotypes and phenocopies. A target-oriented initial search returned historical rescue literature; its biological findings are quarantined from this review's proposals and all competition training, tuning and selection. Protected-target assays belong exclusively to a post-competition study. No target-specific direction, compartment expectation or numerical phenotype is used here.

Confidence is high that replication, composition decomposition and orthogonal assays are necessary, because both source limitations and distinct measurement targets support them. Confidence in any model improvement is low: no empirical comparison exists. Questions for stats: what uncertainty is defensible with one embryo per stage? For devbio: which independently measurable structures distinguish delay from architectural failure without target leakage? For sysbio: what experiment separates regulatory response from compartment loss in your proposed mechanism?
