The hidden scientific assumption is that a change in embryo-average expression reflects regulation within comparable cells. For gene g, the population mean is a sum of state proportions times conditional means. Its change includes a composition term, a within-state term and their interaction. A decreased cardiac marker can therefore indicate fewer differentiated cardiomyocytes, less expression per cardiomyocyte, delayed differentiation, differential survival, or sampling bias. State definitions can themselves move under knockout; conditioning on a post-intervention state can remove a real phenotype or introduce selection bias. Relative abundance cannot establish absolute cell loss without independent counting.

Primary literature supports this caution, not this project's performance. Büttner et al., *scCODA is a Bayesian model for compositional single-cell data analysis*, Nature Communications (2021), published article, [full text inspected](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-27150-6), models relative cell abundances jointly and discusses limitations of discrete state definitions during development. Schiebinger et al., *Optimal-Transport Analysis of Single-Cell Gene Expression Identifies Developmental Trajectories in Reprogramming*, Cell (2019), published article, [full-text PDF inspected](https://www.broadinstitute.org/files/publications/2019/03/1-s2.0-S009286741930039X-main.pdf), explicitly introduces growth/death priors, short-step transport and a Markov assumption. Thus a temporal coupling is an assumption-dependent estimate, not measured ancestry.

Zhao et al., *Loss of both GATA4 and GATA6 blocks cardiac myocyte differentiation and results in acardia in mice*, Developmental Biology (2008), published article, [abstract verified directly](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18400219/), reports redundancy under tetraploid complementation and retained second-heart-field progenitors in double-null embryos. PMC and publisher full-text opens failed, so I do not claim full-text verification. Functional redundancy does not establish induced compensatory upregulation, feedback strength, or the response of this challenge's single knockout.
