# Spatial transcriptomics initial review

My worldview is measurement-first: an expression matrix and point cloud jointly reflect tissue, assay selection, segmentation, and sampling. I prefer independent embryos, imaging landmarks, negative controls, and explicit transformation tests over visually coherent embeddings. The persona at `Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:114–139` correctly directs attention to panels and coordinate frames, but its barcode counts, historical scores, and claimed canonicalization checks are unsupported here. I do not adopt them as evidence.

Inspection of PROJECT_STATE.md, inventory.json, and the supplied sources finds no biological datasets or implemented prediction architecture. The relevant concrete weakness is `Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:175–185`: the proxy takes the first at most 150 rows of both candidate and target and passes their expression and coordinates to an absent scoring module. If storage follows acquisition order, this selects a region rather than a representative embryo. This is a code-level risk, not a demonstrated bias in unavailable data. A strength is exact panel-order validation and finite expression/coordinate checks at `Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:128–147`. They protect file integrity, although they cannot certify biological realism.

The current [official T2 scoring specification](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/evaluation?section=scoring&task=2), read directly on 2026-09-27, describes a 500-gene assay and distinct expression, population, shape/scale, and neighborhood questions. Neighborhood fidelity compares distributions of expression means over 15 spatial neighbors; it does not require cell correspondence. Shape distances operate on coordinates. Consequently, matching expression marginals does not establish their correct spatial placement, and a low neighborhood discrepancy does not establish signaling mechanisms. Neighborhood averaging can erase narrow boundaries. Rigid motion leaves spatial neighbors unchanged, whereas anisotropic distortion can change them. Uniform rescaling preserves neighbors but affects the physical-scale term. These relationships provide useful metamorphic checks before model development.

Hidden assumptions needing explicit audit include treating selected panel genes as representative of all transcriptional biology, assuming equal coordinate units across embryos, and treating every nearest neighbor as a meaningful tissue contact. A Euclidean graph can cross cavities or adjacent folded structures. Segmentation can merge neighboring cells, split one cell into several observations, or attribute transcripts to the wrong cell. Dense regions then contribute disproportionate neighborhood profiles. Without images or segmentation metadata, we can test sensitivity to simulated artifacts but cannot claim their actual frequency or correction. Duplicate identifiers alone would not prove duplicated cells; provenance must distinguish reused labels from identical observations.

Primary literature supports caution rather than a chosen architecture. [Spatiotemporal transcriptomic maps of whole mouse embryos at the onset of organogenesis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-023-01435-6), Nature Genetics, 2023, DOI 10.1038/s41588-023-01435-6, is a published article verified in full-text Results and Methods. It reconstructs embryos from Slide-seq sections, with complete and partial embryos explicitly distinguished. Its spatial domains support joint analysis, but its bead-level assay and reconstruction do not establish transfer to this MERFISH task. [Alignment of spatial genomics data using deep Gaussian processes](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-023-01972-2), Nature Methods, 2023, DOI 10.1038/s41592-023-01972-2, is published; abstract and substantial main-text excerpts were accessible in search, but direct full-text opening failed, so I conservatively record abstract-only verification. GPSA models coordinate warping separately from expression readouts. It motivates separating technical frame correction from biological deformation, not automatically applying nonlinear registration between developmental stages.

I explicitly reject aggressive expression-guided nonlinear registration as a default preprocessing step: it can force changing tissue domains into agreement and erase the developmental signal being predicted. I also reject deriving target size from published anchors. [Current rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules), section 10, permit disclosed external resources subject to protected stage/genotype windows and prohibit recovering target properties from score returns. Literature availability does not authorize every atlas sample for every task.

Concrete improvements are a provenance manifest containing embryo, acquisition, units, panel, and sampling information; reproducible whole-cloud sampling with independent embryo splits; and a diagnostic suite that separately permutes expression-position pairing, applies rigid transformations and controlled scaling, and injects merge/split or cropping artifacts. Always transform coordinates and retain row pairing explicitly. Compare copy-last and a simple regional expression model before adding graph message passing. Score every official component, plus panel-stratified errors and boundary preservation where independent annotations exist. All biological validation remains planned; synthetic tests check metric contracts, not predictive success.

Confidence is high that these audits prevent avoidable interpretive mistakes, moderate that sampling changes proxy rankings, and low for any performance gain until data and the real scorer exist. To spatial3d: which unit corrections can be supported without erasing real growth? To gnn: can your graph avoid crossing tissue voids and beat an equally conditioned non-graph baseline? To scgenomics: how will panel selection and segmentation uncertainty affect inferred state proportions?
