# spatial3d — Initial independent review (2026-09-27)

My worldview is that a geometric model is credible only after separating acquisition coordinates, anatomical orientation, physical dimensions, and changing biological structure. I prefer calibrated imaging metadata, independent anatomical landmarks, replicate embryos, and perturbation tests of scorer invariances over attractive registrations. I read `outputs/research_workflow/PROJECT_STATE.md` and `inventory.json` before reviewing `Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:335–360`. The persona's numerical anisotropy and failed-rescaling stories are assertions without accessible artifacts. Its mandated geometry methods, scorer and experiment logs are absent. They cannot establish either a successful method or a closed scientific question.

The supplied framework has a useful separation between candidate discovery and criticism, but no demonstrated biological model. `Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:27–28` imports absent scoring modules. Its coordinate validation at lines 144–147 checks shape and finiteness, not units or orientation. Its proxy at lines 175–185 takes the first 150 rows; an anatomically ordered file could therefore produce a regional rather than tissue-wide comparison. These are concrete weaknesses in the available source, not measured explanations for any leaderboard gap.

The current [official T2 scoring specification](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/evaluation?section=scoring&task=2), inspected as full web text, assigns separate quarters to expression, population, geometry and local organisation. D2 compares RMS-normalised pair distances; occupancy uses PCA/RMS canonicalisation and a 16³ grid; scale uses signed log RMS ratios, scored by absolute distance. The 15-neighbour expression average distribution exercises coordinate/expression pairing without target cell correspondence. These descriptors do not establish topology. Laterality is explicitly a blind spot. PCA ambiguity near repeated eigenvalues deserves a conformance test: axis ordering instability can affect occupancy even when pair distances remain stable. The webpage's proper-sign-flip explanation and blanket mirror-equivalence statement deserve implementation inspection before assuming exact reflection behaviour. The referenced official scorer is not supplied here, so this remains a specification/implementation question. No weight or target morphology should be reverse-engineered from published anchors.

Physical growth is not a coordinate-frame choice. Translation and rotation alter neither physical RMS size nor anatomy; an unknown pixel-to-micron conversion changes apparent growth. Isotropic normalisation is appropriate for a shape descriptor but destroys size information if reused as the submitted physical coordinates. Conversely, anisotropic whitening changes morphology and can erase real elongation. Proposed scale modelling must retain raw calibrated coordinates and separately record origin, handedness, axis spacing, section thickness, cropping, and segmentation exclusions. Without this metadata, differing RMS radius may represent biological growth, unequal tissue coverage, or acquisition distortion. Cell-count freedom also does not imply sampling-density freedom: occupancy and nearest-neighbour averaging can respond to segmentation or regional coverage.

[STalign: Alignment of spatial transcriptomics data using diffeomorphic metric mapping](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-43915-7), Clifton et al., Nature Communications 14:8123 (2023), DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-43915-7, is a peer-reviewed primary paper verified in full web text. It supports accounting for partial tissue matching and nonlinear acquisition distortions, with independent landmark and expression checks. It does not establish developmental extrapolation. My inference is that smooth invertible registration should be a nuisance model only where anatomical correspondence is justified: it cannot explain appearance of genuinely new structures merely by transporting an old cloud. I also located PASTE2, *Partial alignment of multislice spatially resolved transcriptomics data*, Genome Research (2023), https://genome.cshlp.org/content/33/7/1124.full.pdf; publisher access returned 403, so its retrieved search text is snippet-only and contributes no decisive evidence.

I explicitly reject anchor-derived target rescaling and unconditional diffeomorphic interpolation. The former turns evaluation metadata into an unsupported estimate of hidden anatomy; the latter assumes correspondence and conserved structure that snapshots do not identify. A useful initial improvement is a scorer conformance suite on asymmetric synthetic clouds: translation, proper rotation, isotropic scaling, reflection, axis degeneracy, anisotropic distortion, random subsampling, and independent expression-row permutation. Joint row permutation should preserve all metrics; expression-only permutation should isolate local organisation while preserving expression and geometry marginals. Use fixed seeds and sampling for the diagnostic, and treat any unchanged neighbourhood score as an empirical possibility, not an automatic failure.

When permissible data arrive, compare calibrated copy-last against separately fitted scale and morphology hypotheses using held-out whole embryos. Report landmark uncertainty, registration residuals, RMS size, regional coverage, anatomical boundaries, and component/cavity summaries alongside official metrics; none alone proves a mechanism. [Official rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules), full text inspected, allow disclosed external resources subject to protected stage/genotype windows. Anatomical priors require provenance screening under those rules.

Confidence is high that coordinate provenance and metric conformance are necessary; confidence in improvement from any geometric predictor is low because data, scorer and replicate evidence are absent. Questions for spatialtx: which units and section distortions can acquisition metadata identify? For devbio: where do new cardiac structures make correspondence biologically invalid? For sciml: how will you preserve chirality and physical scale while testing, rather than assuming, the scorer's invariances?
