# Scientific machine learning review — initial round

My worldview is that an inductive bias earns its place by specifying a defensible constraint and surviving a counterfactual control. I prefer explicit observation operators, units, matched-capacity ablations, numerical convergence checks and independent biological measurements. The SciML persona asks which invariances are real or already canonicalized (Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:418–442). Its claimed historical chirality corroboration is unverified; neither the cited skill implementation nor the historical anisotropy artifact exists in the inventory. I cannot infer a useful symmetry from that story.

The audited state contains orchestration, not a trained developmental model or biological dataset. Consequently every proposed predictive benefit is a hypothesis. The source's conservation-law remit (Neurl IPS 2026/agenticprompt:315–329) and smooth-latent-state hypothesis (Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:1193) are research questions, not established embryo properties. A major strength is its explicit no-prior, known-prior, learned-prior and randomized-prior comparison (Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:2042–2055). This distinguishes biological information from generic regularization, provided capacity and tuning are matched.

The immediate weakness is an unspecified map from biology to recorded rows. Write a prospective observation model: latent tissue state generates cells; state-dependent capture, dissociation or segmentation selects rows; a panel projection, count/noise model and declared normalization produce expression; a calibrated frame produces coordinates. Document which terms are measured, estimated from training samples or unknown. Changing recovered cell proportions can reflect sampling as well as birth, death and differentiation. Normalized probability mass summing to one is a mathematical convention; it does not imply conserved cell number, RNA amount or tissue volume. The supplied streaming validator checks nonnegative finite values, gene order and coordinate shape (Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:128–147). Those are valid file contracts but cannot establish the observation model or physical conservation.

I explicitly reject adding a hard mass-conserving continuity equation or universal spatial smoothness penalty as a default “physics-informed” improvement. Development includes proliferation, death and tissue boundary formation; these need source terms and state-specific assumptions. Even unbalanced transport does not identify growth when capture varies. Smoothness may suppress genuine boundaries, while a decoder can make many different latent paths observationally equivalent. A successful solver and a unique regularized optimum establish numerical behavior, not a uniquely identified biological trajectory.

Relevant primary literature clarifies this distinction. Schiebinger et al., *Cell* (2019), published version, DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2019.01.006, was verified in the [full publisher PDF hosted by Broad](https://www.broadinstitute.org/files/publications/2019/03/1-s2.0-S009286741930039X-main.pdf), particularly page 3. Waddington-OT explicitly treats independent snapshots as losing temporal coupling, introduces a short-distance assumption, and modifies conservation constraints for growth/death. These premises must be audited rather than silently imported. Klein et al., *Nature* (2025), published version, DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-08453-2, was verified from [indexed full-text main/methods content](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08453-2) returned by search; subsequent direct opening failed and PMC returned a browser challenge. Moscot describes probabilistic couplings, optional growth/death marginals, and minimum phenotypic displacement/Markov assumptions. It is a relevant candidate, not evidence that this project predicts well. Two search queries were used; no project result is inferred from either publication.

The prospective EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md:19–29 correctly preserves paired rows, separates physical scale from frame correction, and admits that sparse stages do not supply arbitrary temporal validation. Its factorial design and graph randomization controls (lines 11–17, 39) are useful. Add a prior register stating biological domain, observation dependence, hard versus soft enforcement, eligible provenance and a falsification criterion. Test rigid transformations only after inspecting the versioned scorer; do not erase scale or handedness merely because one component is insensitive. Official [rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules), section 10, permit disclosed external resources but exclude protected stage/genotype windows; a prior derived from their measured responses is ineligible.

Validation should first test row-permutation invariance, paired expression-coordinate serialization, unit transformations and controlled clipping. For learned dynamics, vary solver tolerance/step size and report numerical stability separately from held-out forecast effects. For each biological prior, compare no prior, real prior and degree-preserving randomized priors using training-only construction and the same budget. Independent-stage/embryo effects on raw MMD and expression/spatial diagnostics are needed for predictive benefit; lineage or orthogonal assays are needed for mechanism. Synthetic tests cannot substitute for either.

Confidence is high that this audit prevents unsupported interpretations, but low about which prior improves forecasting because data and scorer are absent.
