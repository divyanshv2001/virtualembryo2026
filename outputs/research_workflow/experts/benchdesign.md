# Benchmark Design — initial independent review

My worldview treats validation access as a limited resource and every score as the output of a measurement contract. Preferred evidence is a versioned scorer, auditable sample provenance, matched baselines and outcomes from a holdout that did not choose the model. Persona history is not evidence: the quota assertions in `Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:471–495` need fresh verification. I read PROJECT_STATE.md and inventory.json; this workspace contains orchestration, not biological datasets or trained predictors. All model benefits below remain hypotheses.

The most consequential implementation finding is `Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:175–185`: proxy evaluation uses the first 150 rows. Row order can encode sample, region or cell state, so this measures an arbitrary subset rather than a reproducible whole-cloud estimate. Lines 188–195 hardcode purported real scores without accompanying artifacts. Lines 227–235 admit named “documented winners”; lines 251–266 prefer names rather than independently authenticated evidence. The proxy-minus-real comparison at line 248 combines different splits and cannot establish improvement. Strengths include streaming format validation at lines 128–147 and explicitly describing the proxy as nonauthoritative at lines 237–240. However, absent `common` and `scoring` imports at lines 27–28 prevent biological execution.

The prospective design has substantial strengths: `outputs/research_workflow/EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md:11–19` separates composition, state and spatial change; lines 25–29 acknowledge the actual limits of stage and genotype coverage; lines 33–41 distinguish Monte Carlo seeds from biological replication and preserve insufficient evidence as unknown. Withholding one of T1's two released stages leaves one time point: persistence can be assessed, a two-point learned trend cannot. A Mab21l2 holdout is one gene transfer case, not validation of broad unseen-gene generalization. Random cell splits solve neither problem. Additional resources must pass the current protected-window and genotype exclusions, including pretrained exposure, described in [official rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules) (2026, full page verified).

The hidden assumption at design line 21 is that residual diagnosis can choose an architecture without consuming validation information. If the residual comes from the final proxy target, choosing OT, flow or a graph model is adaptive model selection. Equal tuning budgets do not restore independence. Freeze a decision tree using training evidence or separate development embryos; log every diagnostic query and candidate, then evaluate the chosen recipe once on untouched assessment data. If no further stages or embryos exist, report this as an exploratory comparison, not an unbiased selected-model estimate. Local holdouts must also have a declared reference stage and forecast horizon; the current `_proxy_spec` at lines 150–159 is not an interpolation design merely because the board name contains interpolation.

Versioning is a prerequisite, not another architecture. Record scorer commit/hash, panel order, normalization, sampling policy, probe fitting, anchors and raw components alongside any aggregate. [Official T3 evaluation](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/evaluation?section=scoring&task=3) (2026, full page verified) scores response recovery, direction, magnitude and state distribution; geometry is not scored, although coordinates remain required. Its target-trained frozen probe belongs to the evaluator; a local target-fitted diagnostic probe must never enter predictor fitting. Negative controls should include matched wild type, a gene-agnostic generic shift, sign reversal, expression-level scaling, covariance destruction and expression-position permutation. Prespecify which metric each corruption should affect; spatial controls are relevant to T2, while T3 geometric fidelity requires a separate scientific endpoint.

Recent primary literature reinforces baseline discipline. [Ahlmann-Eltze, Huber and Anders, Nature Methods 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02772-6?error=cookies_not_supported), published article, full HTML verified, compares deep models with simple linear predictors and warns against assuming complexity yields improvement. [Viñas Torné et al., Systema, Nature Biotechnology, online 2025 / volume 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-025-02777-8?error=cookies_not_supported), published article, full text verified, shows shared perturbation variation can inflate conventional performance and motivates perturbation-specific controls. [Wei et al., Nature Methods, online 2025 / volume 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02980-0), published analysis, abstract and availability sections verified only, benchmarks 27 methods across 29 datasets and six metrics under multiple generalization scenarios. These are evidence about evaluation methods, not this project's performance.

I reject tuning perturbation amplitude by solving published skill anchors or differencing returned scores to reconstruct the target; the official rules prohibit such recovery. Ordinary selection among precomputed predictions is distinct. I also reject requiring one complex shared architecture before simple per-task baselines establish a deficit. Match gene panels, preprocessing, samples, validation access and tuning budgets for persistence, convex mixtures, shrunk affine/low-rank shifts and composition-only controls; use training-only identities for all fitted quantities.

Confidence is high in these design criticisms because they follow directly from inspected contracts and code; confidence in any predictive gain is unknown without data. Questions for stats: what independent assessment remains after residual-based selection? For scgenomics: can a generic shift control distinguish gene identity from maturity/composition? For biovalid: which independent replicated endpoint can assess T3 morphology outside the leaderboard?
