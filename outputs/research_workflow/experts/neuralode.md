I prefer held-out physical-time predictions, independently measured lineages or velocities, and explicit sensitivity to growth, representation and solver choices. Snapshot marginals constrain distributions at sampled times. They do not uniquely specify cross-time cell couplings or velocities. Even knowledge of a continuous density path and a continuity equation leaves ambiguity: adding a flux whose divergence is zero preserves the density evolution under suitable boundary conditions. With growth or death, transport and source terms introduce further ambiguity. Sparse marginals constrain still less. Smoothness, minimum kinetic energy, a potential landscape, or a known gene network select a solution class; they are substantive priors, not discoveries obtained merely by fitting snapshots. A learned latent coordinate system can also change the apparent velocity and its scale. The appropriate identified target may therefore be predictive marginals under declared assumptions, with an ensemble of plausible dynamics, rather than recovered cellular trajectories.

Three precise literature searches were run; no live project APIs were used. [Tong et al., TrajectoryNet](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8320749/) is a published ICML/PMLR 2020 paper, verified in full-text sections 3–4. It explicitly addresses underconstrained continuous normalizing flows through energy and Jacobian penalties, density priors, optional velocity information and growth. This supports specifying regularization and extra evidence; it does not certify biological identification. [Yeo et al., PRESCIENT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-23518-w), published Nature Communications 2021, was verified through its full-text introduction, results and methods returned by search; a subsequent open failed. It uses a stochastic potential model, considers proliferation, and evaluates fate predictions against lineage tracing. Its assumptions and orthogonal validation are relevant templates, not results for this absent project. [Chen et al., Neural Ordinary Differential Equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07366), 2018 preprint, was verified abstract-only; it establishes solver-based continuous dynamics architectures, not biological validity. A current primary paper, [Dou et al., DynNet](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-73257-z), published Nature Communications on 19 May 2026, was inspected in full-text methods. It explicitly builds Hill-function regulatory assumptions and stochastic dynamics into its model. Mechanistic structure therefore must be stated and challenged, rather than inferred from the term Neural ODE.

I reject the tempting blanket exclusion of OT, flow matching, bridges and Neural ODEs based on an alleged negative consecutive-displacement cosine. An autonomous field can have changing directions along a curved state trajectory; population means can reverse because mixture weights change. Such a statistic may falsify constant population-displacement continuation in a chosen representation, not all state-dependent or time-dependent dynamics. Conversely, I also decline immediately training a large Neural ODE and advertising learned developmental mechanisms.

Concrete improvements are a scoped falsification protocol and a separate numerical audit. Once authorized data exist, use one training-only embedding, actual time intervals, embryo-level uncertainty, matched cell-state analyses, and composition-controlled displacement estimates. Compare persistence, regularized linear continuation, autonomous dynamics and time-dependent dynamics using distinct interior and later-time holdouts. Passing an interior holdout does not validate extension outside observed time support. If only three stages are available, one pseudo-future holdout provides limited evidence and architecture selection must remain modest.

Numerical checks should tighten tolerances and change solvers while holding parameters fixed, recording prediction changes and function evaluations; where feasible compare training gradients from direct differentiation and adjoint methods. Biological checks instead require correct fate/composition, expression distributions and independent-embryo generalization. A converged integrator can faithfully solve a wrong model; a biologically useful model can still be stiff. Confidence is high in the nonidentifiability and scope objections, moderate in the usefulness of the proposed audits, and unassigned for model superiority because nothing has been tested here.
