# Representation Learning review — replearn
Date: 2026-09-27. Initial review; biological experiments are planned.

My worldview is that a representation earns its complexity through predictive transfer in the observation space actually scored. I prefer embryo-separated downstream tests, conditional probes, decoder diagnostics and auditable preprocessing to attractive latent plots. My main failure modes are mistaking reconstruction for forecasting, discarding rare developmental programs, and confusing acquisition or dissection variables with biological time.

The inventory and PROJECT_STATE establish that no biological data, checkpoints or training implementation are present. The persona's cited autoencoder experiment and probe gains are unsupported historical premises, not evidence: Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:365–382 cites absent files. Its underlying question remains useful: what downstream consumer benefits from sharing a latent space? Neurl IPS 2026/agenticprompt:278–292 explicitly asks about shortcuts; Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:263–290 asks for raw, PCA and learned representations but lists smoothness and reconstruction without a decisive forecast criterion. Strengths are the requested ablations (prompts.md:1974–2004) and leakage inventory (prompts.md:1552–1600). Weaknesses are the unbounded architecture menu and absence of a reproducible experiment contract.

Three latent shortcuts need separate probes: technical depth/detection, embryo/assay identity, and tissue composition. Stage predictability alone is ambiguous because stage and dissection can covary. The official data page says T1 is a heart-centred dissection with changing surrounding tissue, and label vocabularies are not harmonised. I would probe stage within harmonised cell types and separately predict embryo, assay, nonzero fraction and supplied acquisition metadata; derive expression summaries without calling log-normalised sums raw library size. Conditional nuisance predictability is a diagnostic, not proof of causation, and removing every stage-correlated feature could erase real development. Source: [official data](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/data), inspected directly.

Assay alignment is a hidden assumption. T1 has 32,285 genes in dissociated scRNA-seq; T2/T3 measure a 500-gene MERFISH panel. A pretrained transcriptome model may interpret unmeasured genes as biological zeros or use token ranks that change when only a panel is available. Record measured-gene masks, vocabulary coverage, species mapping and input transform. Compare models using the same observed genes and the same decoder/output scale; do not credit a shared encoder for a different observation model. Exact output order and finite nonnegative values are enforced by Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:128–147; the scorer import is absent at :28, so this wrapper does not verify the full metric implementation.

Literature gives a reason to test both simple and pretrained features. Cui et al., “scGPT,” Nature Methods (2024), published article, [DOI](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-024-02201-0), abstract-only verification plus directly read data availability, describes pretraining over 33 million cells and downstream adaptation. It does not establish embryo extrapolation or masked MERFISH compatibility. Kedzierska et al., “Zero-shot evaluation reveals limitations of single-cell foundation models,” Genome Biology (2025), published, [full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13059-025-03574-x), directly inspected results/methods, compares Geneformer/scGPT with HVG, scVI and Harmony, finding weaknesses in zero-shot clustering and batch handling. This is evidence against assuming universal transfer, not against fine-tuning.

Two current sources sharpen that caution. “Parameter-free representations outperform single-cell foundation models on downstream benchmarks,” [arXiv:2602.16696](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16696v1), 2026 preprint, full-text methods inspected, reports competitive linear pipelines and explicitly applies training-fitted ANOVA/PCA to test data. Its annotation results do not prove future-state prediction. Chen et al., “Harmonised benchmarking…context-dependent generalisation,” [arXiv:2607.17227v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17227v2), September 2026 preprint, abstract-only, reports modality/preprocessing-dependent rankings; no single winner. None measures this absent project's performance.

I propose a small matched bake-off: released log-expression plus regularised prediction; training-only PCA with a fixed dimension grid; a small autoencoder; and one provenance-approved frozen pretrained encoder. Fit feature selection, scaling, batch adaptation and all trainable transforms inside each training fold; use inner validation for choices. Keep predictor capacity and output sampling matched. Report expression distribution metrics, per-type marker trajectories and rare-state recall, with paired uncertainty across independent embryos when replication exists. Never refit evaluation PCA, bandwidths or cell-type classifier on each candidate. Distinguish training representation from scorer space: evaluator-internal target fitting, if prescribed by the official implementation, is not permission to expose targets to training. The task-specific evaluation endpoint failed to load during this review, so exact PCA/classifier details remain to be verified.

A finite decoder can lose future support even when its training reconstruction is excellent. Diagnose held-out released-stage reconstruction, residual marker programs and out-of-support rejection; examine whether predictions merely resample existing types. No encoder can identify genuinely unseen developmental states without additional assumptions. I reject immediately adopting a unified pretrained developmental latent and claiming transfer from smoothness or MSE. External resources are allowed with disclosure, but protected stages/genotypes are forbidden even through pretrained weights: [rules §10](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules), read directly. Unknown corpus provenance must remain unresolved, not silently approved.

Confidence is high in the need for these controls, low in any representation ranking before data.
