# Graph Neural Networks — initial independent review

My worldview is that a graph is an explicit assertion about which observations may exchange information. I prefer provenance records, topology interventions, and matched predictive comparisons to architecture names. A spatial edge asserts proximity; a coexpression edge asserts association; a curated regulatory edge may assert a directed interaction in a specific context. These are different claims. Neither attention weights nor good prediction turn association into regulation.

I read PROJECT_STATE.md and inventory.json before inspecting the sources. The supplied tree contains orchestration, not a trained GNN or biological data. `Neurl IPS 2026/agenticprompt:243–257` explicitly requests construction, oversmoothing, leakage and topology scrutiny. `Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:367–386` proposes gene, pathway and learned graphs with a prior/no-prior comparison; `Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:394–413` proposes spatial models using coordinates and expression. These are useful experimental ingredients, but no executed comparison is present. `Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:308–331` supplies my lens while asserting a failed naive kNN baseline and a specific neighborhood metric. Its referenced implementation and historical result files are absent. I reject those assertions as project evidence, including the implication that any graph family is already falsified.

The source's strength is recognizing that biological priors and graph leakage need scrutiny. Its weakness is treating a list of graphs as if edge semantics, deployment inputs and evaluation contracts were settled. Hidden assumptions include availability of target coordinates before prediction, expression homophily across tissue boundaries, comparable coordinate units across specimens, enough independent embryos to tune topology, and useful coverage of the perturbation gene in the prior. None is demonstrated locally.

My first improvement is an edge provenance contract. Every graph must record node type, allowed feature sources, embryo/stage/genotype, coordinate transform, graph rule, direction, weighting, self-loops, distance cutoff, and preprocessing fit split. Construct cell graphs independently within each training embryo; never concatenate training and held-out cells before PCA, neighborhood search or normalization. For inference, distinguish edges on supplied inputs from edges on generated positions/features. Evaluator-only target graphs must remain outside the learner. Current [official rules](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules), section 10, permit disclosed licensed external resources but exclude protected stages/genotypes and inherited pretrained exposure; a prior requires its own source and exposure audit. The source's blanket historical prohibitions cannot replace this contract.

Euclidean kNN can connect opposing walls of a cavity because straight-line distance does not encode an intervening lumen. Fixed k also produces long edges in sparse regions. This is a geometric risk, not an observed defect here. Compare mutual-kNN plus an explicit radius against ordinary kNN, inspect edge-length tails and boundary-crossing edges, and use a toy hollow shell to expose shortcuts. Tissue-aware geodesics need independently supplied anatomy; inferring the boundary from held-out expression would introduce another leakage route. Synthetic checks establish implementation behavior, not biological improvement.

Topology and model capacity need separate controls. Compare a feature-only MLP, coordinate-conditioned MLP, fixed neighborhood averaging followed by the same predictor, and a shallow message-passing model under comparable parameter and tuning budgets. Include degree-preserving rewiring and edge removal, documenting whether spatial length distributions are preserved. Rewiring tests sensitivity; it does not alone prove a biological mechanism. Sweep small depths and report neighborhood feature collapse, cell-type boundary mixing and rare-state retention alongside downstream metrics. Residual paths can reduce collapse but do not validate incorrect edges.

[Wu et al., Simplifying Graph Convolutional Networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/wu19e.html), ICML 2019, is a published paper verified here at abstract level: it motivates a fixed smoothing control by reducing GCNs to a low-pass filter plus classifier. [Li, Han and Wu, Deeper Insights into Graph Convolutional Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.07606), AAAI 2018 (arXiv record confirms venue), was verified at abstract level and identifies Laplacian smoothing and deep oversmoothing concerns. Neither proves that an MLP wins this challenge. [Roohani, Huang and Leskovec, GEARS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-023-01905-6), published online 2023, Nature Biotechnology volume 2024, was verified in full-text main results: separate coexpression and GO graphs support perturbation prediction, while poorly connected unseen genes limit transfer. Its substantial perturbation screens differ from the absent embryo implementation and sparse knockout setting; its scores cannot be imported here.

Validation must hold out whole embryos, fit preprocessing on training only, freeze topology tuning before final evaluation, and report paired replicate-level metric differences with identical sampling. Require gains beyond smoothing and capacity controls, topology sensitivity consistent with the claimed edge meaning, and no loss of rare cell states. Check official neighborhood fidelity separately from DE direction and distribution fidelity; the local 15-NN statement is not a verified scoring implementation. Confidence is high that these controls prevent unsupported attribution, low that any GNN improves the task before data arrive. I explicitly decline immediate deep spatial-GNN or GEARS adoption.
