# Loop 2 correction — Benchmark Design

**C2-2 accepted: contrast renamed and attribution narrowed.** The primary comparator is `endpoint_mixture`; the proposed transformation is `shrunk_endpoint_recentering`. Both contain temporal conditional-expression information. D is MMD(endpoint mixture,target) minus MMD(shrunk endpoint recentering,target). A future positive D would support this frozen transformation on E6.75/E8.0 → E7.25, not isolated composition/state error attribution, causal state dynamics or generalization to official targets. Persistence remains secondary.

For a stratum with equal endpoint proportions, donor weights q_A=.6 and q_B=.4, and endpoint means a,b, the mixture mean is m=.6a+.4b. Recentering by x' = x + λ(m−μ_s) preserves that conditional mean because .6(m−a)+.4(m−b)=0. The between-endpoint covariance changes from .24(a−b)(a−b)^T to (1−λ)^2 times that quantity; λ=.5 gives one quarter. Within-endpoint residual covariance is unchanged by these constant shifts before the nonlinear output map. Thus a distributional gain can occur without a mean change.

With unequal proportions p_Ak=.9 and p_Bk=.1, q_A=.6×.9/(.6×.9+.4×.1)=27/29 and q_B=2/29. For a=0,b=10, the donor mixture mean is 20/29, whereas the stage-weighted center m is 4. At λ=.5 the new conditional mean is .5(20/29)+.5(4)=68/29. Mean and between-endpoint variance both change. These are symbolic arithmetic illustrations before the output map, not biological measurements; nonlinear normalization can alter both further. Correct donor weighting and implementation checks belong to genmodel's companion correction.

**C2-5 accepted: specification is not enforcement.** The template explicitly records policy specified, illustrative sequence present, enforcement absent and untested, clean-context isolation absent and untested. Execution remains gated. The earlier JSON/boolean assertions checked declared values only: no actual repeat-assessment refusal, immutable lock protection, durable ledger or excluded-artifact read was exercised. Future operational verification must call these operations against persisted model/data/config hashes and observe rejection. The hand-written final event now says what policy would require, rather than reporting an actual refusal. No production harness is claimed or added.

**C2-6 template portion accepted.** Separate readiness predicates now cover descriptive expression, confirmatory expression and spatial analysis. Descriptive secondary components carry no noninferiority claim, so η_j and simultaneous guardrail procedures are conditional/not applicable; their unknown numerical values remain null. Confirmatory expression additionally requires independent biological replication, untouched assessment, justified δ*, alpha and inference rules. Spatial interpretation has its own frame/units/coverage gate and never blocks a valid expression-only analysis. Until that gate passes, donor coordinates are serialization placeholders excluded from anatomical, joint and neighborhood claims.

Residual risks include unavailable samples/scorer, incomplete normalization and annotation contracts, unrepresentative proxy stages, capture bias and unresolved exposure. Revised schema semantics do not implement durable access control. No outcome, numerical pilot margin, readiness or biological result is asserted. These are final second-loop corrections; no third critic is invoked.
