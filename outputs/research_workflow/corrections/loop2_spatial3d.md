# Loop 2 correction — spatial3d, C2-3 and spatial side of C2-4

**Disposition: accept C2-3; adopt independent readiness gates.** Expression fidelity and spatial fidelity are separate. A predictor may have a defined expression distribution while its pooled coordinate cloud is anatomically uninterpretable. Donor-row pairing only preserves each sampled cell's original expression/position association. It cannot align independent embryo origins, rotations, handedness, units, coverage or stage-specific anatomy. The finite-array check at `Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:144–147` establishes none of these properties.

**Expression readiness.** Before the primary expression-only released-stage proxy analysis, freeze the corrected endpoint-mixture versus shrunk endpoint-recentering estimator, donor weighting, measured panel, normalization/denominator contract, annotations, independent specimen manifest, preprocessing, MMD implementation and access policy. Expression MMD must not require a common spatial frame. Passing the spatial gate is neither necessary nor sufficient for expression readiness. Current missing data, normalization and scorer information still prevent declaring numerical expression assessment ready.

**Spatial readiness.** Pooled coordinates can support an interpretable spatial analysis only after a training-derived common-frame contract passes. Record physical units and per-axis spacing; frame origin, axis convention and handedness; the permitted acquisition or anatomical landmarks used to align each training embryo; registration procedure/version and transforms; field-of-view, tissue-coverage and missing-section masks; residual uncertainty; and the anatomical correspondence assumed across stages. Fit every transform without E7.25 assessment truth, expression, geometry, landmarks or masks. A target-informed registration is assessment adaptation even if expression prediction remains unchanged. Training-derived centring or PCA orientation alone is not evidence of a biologically shared frame or homologous tissue support.

Common coordinates are necessary, not sufficient: pooling repeated structures from different embryos may create duplicated anatomy, artificial density and cross-donor neighbours even after correct registration. Duplicate sampling, region weighting, tissue coverage, neighborhood reconstruction and the intended population/spatial estimand must also be prespecified. No metric invariance can repair mixing inconsistent frames because the operation changes internal geometry rather than merely applying one rigid transform to the whole cloud.

**Failure branch.** Until the common-frame and support contracts pass, spatial readiness is false. Retained donor coordinates, if required for serialization, are placeholders excluded from anatomical, joint-distribution, neighbourhood, shape and growth interpretations. Describe the model as an expression predictor with retained donor-coordinate fields. Do not report its official-looking spatial outputs as meaningful descriptive results. Alternatively, a per-embryo spatial analysis needs a separately frozen estimand, donor construction and scoring contract before assessment access; it is not an automatic substitute.
