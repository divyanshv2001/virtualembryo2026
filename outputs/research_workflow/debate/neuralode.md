I endorse devbio's refinement of neuralode-r1-2. Held-out mmd_u and de_direction support only the particular distributional observables they measure, conditional on sound splits, preprocessing and sampling. They do not establish progenitor-to-descendant correspondence, individual trajectories, growth/death rates, or a regulatory mechanism. The phrase “held-out embryo metrics test predictive dynamics” in my proposal was too broad; I have changed it to “test marginal forecast utility.” The question and endorsement were received through the parent's attributed relay and verified by reading `outputs/research_workflow/debate/devbio.md`; no response is inferred from a persona.

The distinction is mathematical as well as biological. Several couplings can share the same endpoints, and distinct vector fields can generate the same density path. Unobserved source and sink processes can further trade off against transport. A low distributional discrepancy therefore cannot choose the correct lineage coupling. Conversely, independent growth/death measurements alone cannot establish a coupling either: they reduce a specific ambiguity. I agree that withheld clonal descendant data plus independent proliferation/death evidence provide a substantially stronger fate test. These are evidentiary requirements for our proposed validation design, not a universal assertion that every possible fate assay must use exactly these technologies.

The resolving experiment is to fit competing autonomous and time-dependent fields on identical authorized snapshots, with an embedding fit only to training embryos. Fix selection and tuning before revealing tracing outcomes. Evaluate held-out embryo marginals first under matched sampling. For models with practically equivalent marginal predictions, evaluate progenitor-conditioned descendant fate distributions against withheld clones and compare predicted abundance changes against independent growth/death estimates. Account for clone relationships and embryo nesting when estimating uncertainty; clones from one embryo do not create independent embryos. A model that matches marginals but fails this test retains a marginal forecasting claim and loses its fate claim. Tracing agreement supports the tested fate relationship, without identifying a unique regulatory mechanism.

Disposition: retain neuralode-r1-1 as a synthetic audit of an exclusion argument; refine neuralode-r1-2's terminology and keep numerical and observable-specific biological checks separate.
