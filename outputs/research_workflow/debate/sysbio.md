# sysbio cross-examination of sciml

I read sciml's actual review and both round-1 proposals. I endorse rejecting hard cell-mass conservation and universal smoothness, but refine sciml-r1-1: documenting an observation operator can distinguish definitions and expose nonidentifiability; it cannot generally distinguish capture changes from biological abundance changes using recovered rows alone. The proposed falsifier should test this limitation explicitly.

Evidence: `Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:128–147` validates file structure, not capture. The first-cell proxy sampling at lines 163–184 can further alter composition. Schiebinger et al., Cell (2019), [full-text PDF, page 3](https://www.broadinstitute.org/files/publications/2019/03/1-s2.0-S009286741930039X-main.pdf), introduces growth/death and transition assumptions rather than obtaining physical abundance from normalized row frequencies. For state s, recovered proportions are proportional to capture probability c_s times physical abundance N_s. Halving N_s with fixed capture and halving c_s with fixed abundance can generate identical observed proportions. No contract can recover which happened without additional information.

Missing data: this workspace has no capture calibration, absolute counts, embryo replicates or biological expression observations. Therefore even a perfectly documented operator would presently contain unknown parameters. My own composition/conditional-expression decomposition also describes the observed population; I will not name its composition term cell loss or its conditional term regulation without further evidence.

Proposed experiment: create two synthetic latent systems with the same observed distributions but different abundance/capture causes, plus a separate conditional-expression shift. The diagnostic should identify which components are observable and abstain on the abundance/capture pair unless independent capture information or counts are supplied. Test state mapping at alternative resolutions and preserve expression-coordinate pairing. This is a planned measurement-identifiability check, not biological validation or model performance.

Questions for sciml: will you replace “distinguish a known capture shift” with an explicit nonidentifiability/abstention requirement when latent systems are observationally equivalent? Which independent measurement identifies physical abundance? What state-specific source terms would justify relaxing conservation, and what boundary measurements would justify any smoothness penalty? For sciml-r1-2, will randomized controls also preserve node evidence density, state specificity and expression support where feasible, rather than only graph degree? Degree preservation alone can change several information channels simultaneously.

Disposition: refine r1-1 and conditionally endorse r1-2 as a planned attribution control. Passing graph controls would support predictive information under that design, not establish feedback, compensation or causality. No experiment was executed and no score gain is asserted.
