# Statistics cross-examination of benchdesign

I endorse benchdesign-r1-1's whole-cloud measurement contract and refine benchdesign-r1-2's independent selection contract. The actual benchdesign review correctly identifies first-row scoring (`Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:175–185`) and the danger of choosing architecture from final-target residuals. `SYNTHESIS_0.md` and `EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md:21,33–35` acknowledge these risks but leave the decision rule incomplete.

Define the primary embryo effect as D = mmd_u(composition-only,target) − mmd_u(combined,target), averaged over a frozen conditional sampling/refit procedure. Freeze one task, horizon, scorer and target-population weighting. For independently replicated assessment, success requires the prespecified one-sided lower confidence bound for the across-embryo mean D to exceed a positive practical margin δ*. Its numerical value must come from eligible pilot evidence or a justified scientific tolerance before assessment; none is defensible from current absent measurements. Persistence remains a secondary comparator. If the headline promises improvement over both baselines, that second superiority requirement must also be preregistered rather than inferred from the primary result.

For each guardrail j, orient G_j positively for deterioration and freeze an acceptable loss η_j. A “without degrading beyond tolerance” claim requires the upper bound for each G_j below η_j, with a declared simultaneous-claim procedure. Include direction, co-expression and applicable T2 shape, scale and neighborhood components. Nonsignificance is insufficient. Secondary exploration can instead remain descriptive, without a noninferiority claim. The official composite is reported separately and cannot substitute for this joint decision.

Paired means both models scored against the same independent target embryo; destructive stage samples are not longitudinal pairs. Audit litter/batch dependence. With one embryo per stage, report D and conditional seed sensitivity descriptively: stage and embryo remain confounded, and seeds/refits do not increase biological n.

No independent assessment remains after final-target residuals choose a model unless a fresh eligible stage/embryo resource is available. Freeze selection using training-only or separate development data, or label the entire comparison exploratory. Eligibility screening precedes resource use. For testing mean D > δ*, planning under anticipated mean μ_A > δ* uses denominator μ_A − δ*, not δ* itself; use the appropriate one-sided critical value and cluster/small-sample sensitivity rather than numerical power here.

Exact questions for **benchdesign**: Which task/horizon is primary, and who freezes δ* and η_j before target access? Does the headline require persistence superiority as well? Which independent assessment survives residual selection? What embryo/litter manifest establishes n? Will the pilot alternative μ_A and success threshold δ* be recorded separately? These are unresolved questions, not an invented peer reply.
