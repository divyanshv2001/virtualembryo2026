# replearn cross-examines biovalid

Actual materials examined: outputs/research_workflow/experts/biovalid.md and outputs/orchestration/round_1/proposals/biovalid.json, specifically biovalid-r1-1 and biovalid-r1-2. Disposition: endorse the distinction between state, composition and mechanism; refine the evidential threshold for representation benefit.

Evidence: biovalid-r1-1 correctly notes that pooled expression agreement can reflect mixture reweighting. This directly constrains my matched representation proposal. Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:263–290 lists latent reconstruction and smoothness, but these cannot discriminate composition-only success from within-type developmental accuracy. Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:175–185 samples the first rows and does not implement embryo-level replication. Neither source supplies a marker-validation result. biovalid-r1-2 correctly separates ancestry and regulation from transcriptomic similarity; adding a good embedding cannot bridge that measurement gap.

Downstream marker support can validate a narrow predictive benefit if marker genes, contrasts, compartments and rejection thresholds are chosen before outcomes; observations come from held-out eligible embryos; and the comparison holds the decoder, predictor capacity and sampling constant. Measure within-compartment marker distributions, not only pseudobulk means, alongside independently measured abundance. If marker selection, compartment assignment and metric tuning all use the same panel and frozen scorer labels, the check is correlated panel validation. It can diagnose predictive errors but is not orthogonal biological confirmation. Frozen assignment avoids adaptation leakage but may still force unfamiliar states into existing labels.

Missing information: eligible embryo counts, batch structure, marker-selection provenance, annotation harmonisation, independent readout availability and an explicit unknown-state policy. With no local data, rare-state preservation and future-state emergence remain hypotheses. Recall among observed training types does not establish support for states absent from training. My initial rare-state diagnostic needs an eligible released-stage holdout containing genuinely withheld populations; otherwise it tests rarity only.

Discriminating test: compare raw/PCA and learned representations through the same prediction head on embryo-separated eligible released-stage holdouts, with a prespecified withheld population where feasible. Score held-out marker distributions and unknown-state detection, then compare independent protein staining, imaging-based compartment counts or separately measured RNA probes if available. Any improvement restricted to decoder reconstruction or training-derived panel annotations defeats a claim of orthogonal benefit. Protected-target observations cannot enter competition selection; official rules §10: https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/rules.

Questions to biovalid: Which marker/readout selection can be fixed independently of the encoder and frozen classifier? How will uncertain compartments and previously unseen states remain unassigned rather than artificially matched? Which orthogonal endpoint specifically distinguishes better state representation from merely better mixture prediction? Can your requirement name a minimum available assay rather than presuppose an unavailable rescue study?
