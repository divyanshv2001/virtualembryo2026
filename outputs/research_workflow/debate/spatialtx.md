# Spatialtx response to actual scgenomics challenge

Position: I accept scgenomics' refinement of `spatialtx-r1-1`. The acquisition-order risk and the effect of rebuilding neighborhoods on a subset are separate. `Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:175–185` selects leading paired expression-coordinate rows before calling an unavailable scorer. It demonstrates selection order, but not where the scorer constructs neighborhoods. My initial sampling remedy was incomplete: seeded sampling eliminates deterministic preference for leading rows, yet can enlarge physical neighborhoods when 15-neighbor means are recomputed after thinning. Neither mechanism has been measured here.

The prospective experiment has two comparisons. First, hold a full cloud fixed, permute its storage order without breaking X-coordinate pairing, and compare leading-row subsets with seeded draws at matched cell counts. Record anatomical coverage, inferred mixture, and every score component. Second, compute neighborhoods on the full cloud once, select the corresponding neighborhood profiles for each subset, and compare these with profiles rebuilt from that same subset. This second comparison isolates neighborhood support changes; it is an auxiliary diagnostic and must not replace official scoring. The full versus subset difference is not exclusively acquisition-order bias.

For the pairing control, freeze type strata before manipulation, retain each stratum's count and coordinate locations, and shuffle entire expression vectors only within strata. Global expression-vector distributions and assigned mixtures then remain fixed, while within-type spatial expression patterns change. This can miss coupling carried entirely by type localization, so a separately labeled between-type shuffle supplies a broader positive control. Recompute classifier outputs separately to expose reclassification, without redefining frozen strata after manipulation.

Segmentation perturbations form a distinct experimental arm. Simulated merges combine neighboring transcript assignments and define an explicit new centroid; splits partition transcripts and define daughter coordinates. Distinguish transcript-conserving reassignment from added-background contamination, and report count, library size, neighborhood support, and classifier changes. These controls reveal sensitivity, not actual artifact prevalence or successful correction.

Missing information: embryo/acquisition IDs, row-order provenance, physical units, registration transforms, segmentation masks and confidence, cell volumes, raw transcript locations/assignments, imaging channels, and scorer preprocessing. Supported correction requires metadata connecting an observed defect to an independent reference—for example known calibration units or reviewed image boundaries. Without that link, use sensitivity analysis and uncertainty reporting.

Disposition: retain both PROCESS proposals with narrowed interpretations. The transformation suite checks controlled metric behavior; it cannot identify biological causes or establish prediction gains. I accept the peer's caution without independently promoting its literature citation to verified evidence.
