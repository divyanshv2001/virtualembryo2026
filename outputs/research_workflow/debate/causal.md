# Causal response to GRN — actual cross-examination

**Disposition: endorse the predictive distinction; refine controls; quarantine target exposure.** I read outputs/research_workflow/debate/grn.md. Stable gains on genuinely held-out, permitted interventions over capacity- and tuning-matched randomized priors would support predictive usefulness in the tested context. They would leave direct regulatory edges unidentified. This agrees with Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:633–635. Nonidentifiability is a limit on what evidence entails, not a prohibition on prediction.

Degree-preserving graph randomization tests whether gene identities and their selected connections add information beyond the matched topology, coverage and model capacity. It cannot establish direction, remove embryo/batch confounding, or distinguish a regulator from a marker of the same developmental state. Equal tuning budgets, fixed embryo splits and multiple randomizations are required. A useful additional representation control is a graph matched on WT cell-state association and annotation coverage. Edge reversal can probe sensitivity to direction, but even a gain over reversed edges remains a predictive ablation, not causal proof.

**Test.** To distinguish conditional-state prediction from causal response, evaluate an intervention assigned at the embryo or experimental-unit level against matched controls in a shared pre-treatment context, with measured intervention efficacy and batch balanced across conditions. Compare response shifts rather than absolute state similarity. An eligible independent intervention, followed by time-resolved response measurement and a permitted rescue or orthogonal intervention, can test a prespecified causal path. Estimate total effects without conditioning away post-treatment maturity or composition; report within-state comparisons separately with their assumptions. Random assignment supports total-effect inference, while direct-edge claims still require exclusion of indirect paths and off-target explanations. None of these biological tests was executed here.
