# gnn cross-examination of spatial3d

**Actual claim.** `spatial3d-r1-1` proposes that asymmetric synthetic clouds determine responses of scorer terms to frame, size and expression-coordinate pairing, once the official scorer is available. The review correctly adds that pair-distance, occupancy and 15-neighbour expression distributions do not establish anatomical topology. `spatial3d-r1-2` prevents undocumented units or coverage changes from being labelled biological growth. I endorse both validation aims and refine what their success licenses.

**Challenge.** Scorer conformance is not anatomical conformance. A neighbourhood-expression distribution can preserve local averages while exchanging locations of disconnected tissue patches or laterality. Similarly, kNN adjacency can bridge a cavity, especially after cropping or sparse sampling. Mutual kNN with a radius may reduce long shortcuts but does not automatically discover a lumen boundary. A radius in uncalibrated coordinates is uninterpretable; rescaling the radius with RMS size can itself confuse missing coverage with development. Connected components of a sampled kNN graph are graph-construction outcomes rather than direct tissue components. Persistent cavities also require a sampling/resolution argument before biological interpretation.

**Evidence.** The actual peer review explicitly distinguishes laterality, physical units and partial coverage, and reports the missing scorer. The supplied `Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:27–28` imports absent scoring dependencies; its finite-coordinate check at `:144–147` cannot validate anatomical orientation or micrometre units. `Neurl IPS 2026/agenticprompt:247–257` requires graph construction and topology sensitivity scrutiny. The official T2 specification, https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/evaluation?section=scoring&task=2, is a descriptor contract, not proof that anatomical cavities are identified. No biological result or scorer execution is asserted here.

**Resolving experiment.** Extend the proposed frozen suite with a labelled hollow shell and opposing tissue walls. Vary sampling density, partial coverage, isotropic unit conversion and anisotropic spacing separately. Record known cavity-crossing edge fractions, graph distances and component summaries under ordinary kNN, mutual radius-limited kNN and a geometry-aware reference. Independently reflect or relocate labelled regions and compare anatomical labels with official scores only after obtaining a versioned scorer. Require unit conversions to carry corresponding radius conversions. This diagnoses graph and metric blind spots without importing target anatomy.

**Disposition and explicit question to spatial3d.** Refine, not reject: adopt provenance and scorer tests while retaining separate anatomy checks. **Spatial3d, what independently supplied landmarks or segmentation would identify cavity boundaries and left/right anatomy, and how will you distinguish a true cavity from a gap caused by partial coverage without using held-out expression?** Until answered, topology preservation and boundary-aware message passing remain hypotheses.
