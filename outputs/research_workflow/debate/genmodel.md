# genmodel cross-examination of diffusion

This is attributed dialogue from the actual saved diffusion review and proposals, not a live exchange or inferred consensus.

**Exact peer claim.** diffusion-r1-1 claims: “A matched temporal benchmark will distinguish a conditional flow's incremental distributional value from gains obtainable by composition-aware simple models.” I refine this claim. Its comparator set includes copy-last, stage-conditioned mixture resampling and a linear latent trend. It must additionally include a conditional-state baseline that preserves residual covariance within each type. Otherwise a flow can win by representing ordinary dispersion omitted from a mean-only trend, leaving its transport assumptions untested.

**My position and evidence.** Empirical resampling already preserves observed joint gene distributions. A fair extension estimates stage-dependent within-type means and scales from training embryos, then resamples whole residual vectors, with shrinkage when populations are sparse. This is a proposed baseline, not a validated model. Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:788–813 explicitly requires baselines before architecture escalation; :819–843 lists conditioning variables but does not establish their availability at prediction. The official [data page](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/data) specifies normalised expression and unharmonised labels. Therefore neither a raw-count likelihood on X nor a categorical decoder whose support equals training labels is justified automatically. A continuous flow can provide flexible multivariate state distributions beyond these comparators, but would establish predictive distribution quality, not newly identified lineages or biological emergence.

**Missing test.** With matched mixture weights, compare residual covariance, tails and conditional mmd_u against the residual sampler. Repeat under different admissible endpoint couplings and training-only representations. A claimed transport advantage must survive coupling sensitivity; identical endpoint scores cannot validate the inferred paths. With sparse time points, identify which withheld-stage tests are actually feasible before promising rolling-origin extrapolation. If only two Task-1 training stages exist, an independent extrapolation evaluation is unavailable without a permitted external series. Sample count is not independent abundance evidence; a growth head needs measured sampling fractions or independently justified constraints. Avoid using target type labels to initialise predicted mixtures.

**Disposition.** Retain diffusion-r1-1 as a PROCESS evaluation specification, conditional on data availability; add the covariance-preserving comparator and explicit support/likelihood contract. Endorse diffusion-r1-2's narrow evidence-status correction: Neurl IPS 2026/orchestration/personas.py:239–248 cites absent stationarity evidence, so blanket falsification is unsupported. That does not justify training a flow now. Architecture escalation remains deferred until a reproducible gain beyond the strongest conditional-state sampler and embryo-level uncertainty is demonstrated.
