# spatial3d response to gnn

**Actual response and disposition: refine.** I accept gnn's distinction between scorer conformance and anatomical validity. Neither proposal licenses topology preservation or boundary-aware message passing. The supplied coordinate check (`Neurl IPS 2026/agent_orchestrator.py:144–147`) lacks anatomical evidence, and the scorer imports at `:27–28` remain unresolved.

The [official T2 contract](https://virtualembryo.ai/challenge/evaluation?section=scoring&task=2) does not provide an explicit cavity-crossing edge penalty. Neighbourhood expression averages might change when edges mix distinct tissues, but identical expression across opposing walls can conceal crossings. Pair distances and occupied voxels can respond to altered cavity geometry without uniquely identifying topology. A coarse occupancy grid can miss small lumens; RMS radius cannot identify them. The specification states a laterality blind spot. These are descriptor limitations, not observed metric results; reflection and PCA ambiguities still require implementation inspection.

**Missing measurements.** A true cavity needs a registered three-dimensional tissue/lumen segmentation from independent structural imaging, such as membrane-labelled imaging or appropriately reconstructed histology, together with the field-of-view mask, missing-section mask, section spacing, resolution, and segmentation uncertainty. Left/right anatomy requires independently annotated landmarks and a recorded handed acquisition frame—for example stage-appropriate anatomical annotations linked to orientation at acquisition. A cloud's principal axes alone cannot supply that frame. Measurements must come from permitted training specimens or lawful resources, without hidden-stage expression or anatomy. If such information is unavailable, a point-free region remains unidentified as cavity versus missing coverage.

**Discriminating test.** Use a synthetic labelled hollow shell and opposing walls with known continuous boundaries. First keep geometry fixed and vary only density; separately remove an acquisition region while retaining its coverage mask. Then vary true lumen width at fixed coverage and density. Independently convert coordinate units together with every physical radius; test anisotropic spacing errors separately. Record the fraction of graph edges whose line segments cross the known lumen under ordinary kNN, mutual radius-limited kNN, and a reference graph constrained by segmentation. Reflection and labelled-region relocation are separate anatomy controls. Repeat across resolutions to establish when lumen detection is identifiable. Compare official metrics only after obtaining a versioned implementation.

A graph component count or persistent cavity without this coverage/resolution evidence is a construction-dependent summary. Passing these controls supports a particular graph's geometric validity; it does not establish biological developmental correspondence or performance on held-out embryos.
