# biovalid responds to replearn

Actual materials examined: debate/replearn.md, experts/replearn.md and round_1/proposals/replearn.json, including replearn-r1-2. I endorse the matched forecast comparison and refine its claim attribution. No assay or model comparison has run.

The minimum independent assay I recommend is multiplexed protein staining with blinded imaging-based compartment counts in eligible independent embryos, using proteins and spatial domains selected before encoder fitting. Selection must follow an externally specified developmental question and an eligibility-audited reference, rather than whichever genes best separate the learned clusters. Independently measured RNA probes outside the competition panel or full-transcriptome profiling can corroborate state, but remain molecularly correlated with panel expression; they are a separate measurement, not universal proof of mechanism. Protein localization and compartment architecture supply a more distinct observation channel. The availability of any such assay is currently unknown.

To distinguish state improvement from mixture prediction, compare prespecified within-compartment protein intensity distributions, co-localization and localization boundaries while separately reporting absolute compartment counts and proportions. Better counts alone support composition accuracy. Better conditional readouts at comparable counts support a narrow state-prediction claim. Neither result establishes ancestry or direct regulation. Use an abstaining compartment classifier with thresholds frozen on eligible training/pilot data. Report unassigned mass, ambiguous domains and disagreement with blinded morphology, rather than relabeling unfamiliar cells into known types. Future support needs a genuinely withheld eligible population with an independently established readout; rarity among known types cannot substitute. Without that population, retain an unknown result.

A matched prediction head is necessary but insufficient for encoder attribution when output decoders differ. Use a shared output mapping where dimension and semantics permit; otherwise cross encoder and decoder choices in a factorial comparison with matched capacity, tuning budgets and output noise. An effect confined to one decoder is an interaction, not a general representation benefit. Include reconstruction and forecasting separately.
