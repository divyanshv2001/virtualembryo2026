# Developmental biology cross-examination of neuralode

The exact claim in neuralode-r1-1 is: “A negative cosine between consecutive population displacements does not by itself falsify autonomous state-dependent dynamics or every transport method; a synthetic counterexample can demonstrate the limitation before data arrive.” I endorse this scoped claim. A rotating autonomous field and changing proportions among stationary states are logically different explanations of centroid reversal. The proposed synthetic audit can expose a defective exclusion rule; it cannot establish which explanation describes an embryo.

The review's further statement that “the appropriate identified target may therefore be predictive marginals under declared assumptions” is the correct developmental boundary. My refinement concerns neuralode-r1-2: later-time mmd_u and de_direction establish forecast utility for measured distributions, conditional on split and preprocessing quality. They do not validate a cellular trajectory, growth law or lineage mechanism, even when solver tolerances converge. Neurl IPS 2026/prompts.md:329–335 lists abundance, conditional expression and lineage together; a report must keep these claims separate.

Schiebinger et al., Cell 2019, DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2019.01.006, [full text verified](https://www.broadinstitute.org/files/publications/2019/03/1-s2.0-S009286741930039X-main.pdf), makes short-transition and Markov assumptions explicit and incorporates growth/death. These assumptions select couplings rather than directly observing them. Weinreb et al., Science 2020, DOI 10.1126/science.aaw3381, [abstract verified](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31974159/), reports fate biases invisible to expression alone. Both are prior methodological evidence, not project results.

The unresolved evidence is lineage correspondence, independent proliferation/death measurements, stage morphology and biological replicate structure. None exists on disk. Even a composition-controlled fit leaves capture efficiency, state annotation and latent-coordinate dependence unresolved.

The resolving experiment would fit competing smooth fields using identical training snapshots and select no model on the tracing outcome. First compare held-out embryo marginals under matched sampling. Among models with equivalent marginal accuracy, compare prospectively withheld clonal descendant distributions from progenitors measured before the interval, alongside independent growth/death estimates. Independent embryos are the uncertainty unit. If these data are unavailable, retain only the distributional prediction claim. Tracing agreement would support the tested fate relationship, not a unique regulatory mechanism.

Provisional disposition: endorse both PROCESS proposals, refine the second to label biological validation by its observable, and reject smoothness or inferred coupling as standalone lineage evidence. No biological experiment has been performed.

Final disposition after actual peer reply: neuralode explicitly narrowed r1-2 to marginal forecast utility and accepted the withheld fate/growth experiment. I endorse that revision. Growth/death measurements constrain source terms but cannot identify ancestry alone; tracing supports only the tested fate relationships. Clone dependence and embryo nesting must remain in uncertainty estimates. Both PROCESS proposals remain endorsed within these limits; bilateral cross-examination is complete.
