## Top five failure modes that would invalidate the direction

1. Conditional-state gains disappear against a frozen covariance-preserving composition baseline under independent embryo/stage assessment; without adequate replication the result stays unknown.
2. The apparent benefit is explained by capture/annotation differences, target-derived preprocessing, embryo/batch confounding or adaptive reuse of the assessed stage.
3. Spatial gains arise from frame/scale/scorer artifacts or matching marginals while losing expression-position dependence and independently observed anatomy.
4. Unseen-gene response predictions depend on protected measured target knowledge, or fail genuinely held-out permitted interventions; one KO cannot justify broad transfer or an identified mechanism.
5. The corrected primary benefit fails its practical margin, exceeds secondary deterioration tolerances, or does not add a documented result beyond closest prior work. Metric success alone would not establish causal novelty.

These are prospective invalidators, not observed failures. Release-stage interpolation cannot settle future-stage extrapolation; logical nonidentifiability limits mechanisms even if a predictor later succeeds. No expert vote or critic verdict supplies empirical replication.
